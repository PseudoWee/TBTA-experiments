Judgement calls and warnings

  • "Song of songs": both versions keep the superlative literally, so it is encoded once. A dynamic "best song" alternate is possible if wanted.
  • Speakers: neither the NIV text nor the NASB gives speaker names in the verse text itself. The woman and the chorus ("Some people") follow the NASB section labels. The chorus's "they" in 1:4 is encoded as "the young women" (the group named in 1:3).
  • Words with no ontology entry: "lovely" is encoded as beautiful, "adore" as love (in the shared line), "flock" as sheep, "chambers" as rooms, "maidens" as young women, "vineyard" as the ontology's own explication gardens of fruit/grapes.
  • Dropped: "own" in 1:6 (rule 0.17).
  • Backtranslation display quirk: after a | or (literal)/(dynamic) pair, the editor's backtranslation shows the following shared sentences under the dynamic reading only. This is how it renders alternates, not a missing sentence in the encoding.
  • Speakers: the NASB gives no heading before 1:8, so 1:8-10 are the man (he is the one who says "my darling"). 1:11 is the chorus (Some people), 1:12-14 the woman, 1:15 the man, 1:16-17 the woman.
  • 1:8: "If you do not know" has no stated object, so it is written do not know-A the place _implicit.
  • 1:9: the dynamic reading drops "I liken", because the simile is already carried by are like.
  • 1:13-14: "my beloved / my lover" follows chapter 2 (the man [whom I(woman) love]). sachet and pouch are written small bag [that has expensive substance [that smells-B good _adj] in the bag].
  • Words with no ontology entry: mare is female horse, bead is jewels, charming/delightful is pleasant, rafter is poles [that support-B the roof], chariot is vehicles/wagons of war, Pharaoh is king/Pharaoh, shepherd is people [who _routinely care for sheep], graze is cause [… to eat grass] (as in 1:7).
  • Exclamations in 1:15-16: "How beautiful you are!" is written as very beautiful!; the repeated "Oh, how …" is a second very … ! sentence.
  • Remaining warnings: henna (1:14), juniper (1:17 literal) and fir (1:17 dynamic) are not in the ontology.

Judgement calls and warnings

  • Speakers (NASB headings): 8:1–3 woman; 8:4 man; 8:5a chorus, then 8:5b–7 woman; 8:8–9 chorus; 8:10–12 woman; 8:13 man; 8:14 woman. 8:5 is therefore two blocks. The chorus says we(people) _excl.
  • 8:1: "if only" is (jussive). Despise is level 2 (hate//laugh at), so laugh at; nursing is drank milk from the breasts.
  • 8:2: give … [to drink] fails (same-participant clause), so it is give wine to you, You would drink that wine, then the pomegranate drink as its own sentence (juice is not in the ontology).
  • 8:3: hug and embrace are level 2, so the literal wish uses hold.
  • 8:4: promise-C has no theta grid, so the literal reading carries the same flag as 2:7 and 3:5.
  • 8:5: "her" becomes that woman. Conceive is not in the ontology (started to have you inside that woman's body). Hyphenated verbs stay uninflected (wake-up-B, come-up), but gave birth is the form used in 3:4 and 6:9.
  • 8:6: seal pairs to mark/sign, and sign fails the level check, so it is a mark. put … like a mark fails (two verbs), so the simile is a separate sentence: I(woman) will be like a mark there. be … Yahweh fails, so the NASB "flame of the Lord" is The flame of Yahweh _noun burns; this is a loose rendering to review.
  • 8:7: quench is put-out (warning). "Many waters" is Much water. The conditional is [If-A …]. give all of the riches fails, so the gift is all of the things [that are in the house of that man]. "It would be despised" is encoded with that man as the object; the Hebrew is ambiguous.
  • 8:8: "spoken for" is [when a man speaks to us … about that sister]; do warns.
  • 8:9: battlement is not in the ontology (small wall); cedar is wood-B/cedar as in 1:17.
  • 8:11–12: vineyard is level 2, so garden of fruit/grapes (as in 2:15). Shekel is not in the ontology (shekels _noun). Solomon is a vocative in 8:12.
  • 8:13: listen fails, so the NASB is friends want [to hear your(woman's) voice].
  • 8:14: gazelle and stag as in 2:17. hurry and come-away warn.
  • Warnings only: 8:1, 8:5D, 8:6 (jealousy, flame), 8:7 (put-out), 8:8 (do), 8:11 (names, shekel), 8:14 (hurry, come-away). The one flag is 8:4 literal (see above); there are no other errors.
