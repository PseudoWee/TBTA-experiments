(begin-poetry)
[v1] Yahweh, I(David) have many enemies! Many people oppose me(David)!
[v2] Many people say about me(David), ["God will not save David"].
[v3] But you(Yahweh), Yahweh, protect me(David). You(Yahweh) honor me(David). And you(Yahweh) encourage me(David).
[v4] (literal) I(David) cried to Yahweh. And Yahweh [who is on Yahweh's special/holy mountain] answered me(David).
(dynamic) I(David) cry to Yahweh. And Yahweh [who is on Yahweh's special/holy mountain] answers me(David).
[v5] (literal) I(David) lie-down _past. And I(David) slept. Then I(David) wake-up _past [because Yahweh supports me(David)].
(dynamic) I(David) lie-down _present. And I(David) sleep. Then I(David) wake-up _present again [because Yahweh supports me(David)].
[v6] I(David) will not be afraid [even-though many thousands of people stand around me(David) [in order to fight me(David)]].
[v7] Yahweh, you(Yahweh) (imp) stand-up! My(David's) God, you(God) (imp) save me(David)!
(literal) For _conj you(Yahweh) hit all of my(David's) enemies on the cheek. And you(Yahweh) broke the teeth of evil people.
(dynamic) You(Yahweh) (imp) hit all of my(David's) enemies on the jaw! You(Yahweh) (imp) break the teeth of evil people!
[v8] Yahweh saves people. I(David) pray-hope [you(Yahweh) will do good things for your(Yahweh's) people].
(end-poetry)
